Experiences

The days in between.

The house is the beginning. What we do with the days around it is the reason people come back to us. We do not sell packages. We ask what you have in mind, and then we make the calls.

How a week with us goes

From the first hour to the last evening

01

Arrival

A car at the airport, the keys already in hand, the fridge full. The first hour sets the tone for the week.

02

Off the map

A vineyard that does not take visitors, a workshop, a private visit after closing. The things we can arrange because of who we know.

03

The table

The restaurants locals book a month ahead, the beach club with the right corner, a lunch that runs into the evening.

04

After dark

A table at the club, the guest list, the driver waiting outside. Nights that need no planning on your side.

05

On the water

A boat for the day with a skipper who knows the coves, or a week on a yacht with a crew who know your name.

06

At the house

A chef for one dinner or the whole stay, a masseuse on the terrace, a nanny, a trainer. The house becomes a hotel that is yours.

07

One number

Whatever comes up, before or during the stay, one person answers. That is the whole service in a sentence.
