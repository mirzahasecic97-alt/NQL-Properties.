For estate agencies

We bring the buyer.
You have the house.

NQL Properties is a Norwegian buyer’s agency. People come to us before they start looking, tell us the budget and the region and what the place has to have, and we go and find it. We act for them, not for the seller, which is why we work with agencies rather than against them.

Ask for a demo

You get an account, not an email

Most introductions arrive as a forwarded message and are lost by Thursday. Ours arrive in a system built for it, which you sign into and work from.

A board of buyers looking in your area

Where they want to be, the budget, bedrooms, land, what the house must have and what would rule it out. Marked hot or warm by how much the buyer has told us, so you read the best briefs first.

Your own buyers, once they agree

Name, email and telephone, in one place, with everything they told us about what they are after. Nothing is copied into a spreadsheet and nothing goes missing between colleagues.

Tell us what you have

Taken on something worth knowing about? Put it in and we will look for a buyer among the people who came to us. No listing, no photographs needed, no exclusivity.

A record of what came of it

Spoke to them, viewing booked, offer made, sold. Two seconds a time, and it becomes the account of what working together has actually been worth to you.

How it works

01

You see the brief, not the buyer

Everything about what they are looking for, and nothing that says who they are. No name, no email address, no telephone number, and not a word they wrote to us in private.

02

You tell us which houses fit

If you have something for them, you say so and name the properties. Other agencies may be answering the same brief. We read them together and put forward whoever has the right house, so you win it on the property rather than on who clicked first.

03

We ask the buyer, by name

We go back to them, say who you are and why we think you can help, and wait. Only if they agree do you get their details. If they say no, or say nothing, you never see them. It is why our clients trust us, and why an introduction from us is warm rather than cold.

What it costs you

Nothing to see the board and nothing to keep the account. We are paid by our own client on a completed purchase, on terms agreed with you before anybody is introduced.

You keep the listing

We act for the buyer. We are not after your vendor, your mandate or your commission.

You choose what to answer

Nothing is pushed at you. If a brief does not suit what you have, you ignore it and nothing happens.

You can leave

Tell us and we close the account. Buyers already introduced stay yours to look after.
