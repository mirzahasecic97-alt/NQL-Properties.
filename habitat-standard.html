The Project

A complete community in Tatlısu

Habitat Standard is an 853-property complex on the Kyrenia East coast, offering studios, bungalows and apartments within a single gated development. It is designed to be lived in year-round rather than visited — everything a household needs sits inside the gates.

Construction begins in January 2026 and completes in December 2028, with the purchase price spread across 36 monthly instalments. Sixty-five per cent of the development is already sold.

Outdoors

Ten kilometres of it, inside the gates

01

Trails and paths

Ten kilometres of mountain trails, plus a four-kilometre running and cycling road laid out in two lanes. Benches, lighting and bus stops run the length of it, so the walk works after dark and the far end is never a problem.

02

Courts and sport

A courts centre with tennis, basketball, paddle tennis and sand beach volleyball. For children and for training there is a playground, a climbing wall and an outdoor gym.

03

Gardens

A Zen garden and a dedicated yoga and meditation area for the quiet end of the day. An organic garden and farmers’ market for produce, and a dog garden for the household members who need somewhere of their own.

04

Water

Outdoor swimming pools across the site and a mini aquapark for the summer months, when the heat makes everything else optional.

Indoors

Wellness, work and evenings

Wellness

An indoor pool, a spa and a massage centre, with separate sauna and steam rooms and ice-shower facilities. Indoor gardens run through the wellness floor.

Work and gatherings

Bookable lounges, a co-working space, and a conference hall that doubles as a cinema room — enough to hold a working day or a private event without leaving the complex.

Eating and evenings

A restaurant and a bar and mist lounge on site, and a roof bar with a skylight area given over to stargazing.

Commercial

The errands, at the doorstep

The commercial offering inside the complex covers 24-hour accident and emergency healthcare, a supermarket, a coffee shop and bakery, a bank and ATM, a pharmacy, car rental, bike and scooter rental, and beauticians.

Payment Terms

36 months, interest-free

Habitat Standard is sold direct from the developer, with the balance spread across 36 monthly instalments, interest-free, through to completion in December 2028. The deposit and the full payment schedule are confirmed per unit type — ask us and we will send the current terms.

Purchase taxes in North Cyprus are calculated on 70% of the declared price and total approximately 14.5% of that figure — see the full cost breakdown.
