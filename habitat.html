The Project

Relocate to paradise

An extraordinary opportunity at the stunning complex in Tatlısu — a selection of studios, bungalows and apartments built for a luxurious and harmonious way of living.

The scheme runs to 853 properties across a single master-planned community, with the outdoor and indoor facilities of a resort rather than a residential block: 10 km of mountain trails, a 4 km running and biking road, a courts centre, spa and wellness floors, and a commercial strip that covers everything from healthcare to a bakery.

Convenience and security run through the whole site — lit paths, benches along the routes, bus stops for public transport, and on-site services open around the clock.

On the Masterplan

A resort, not a block

The outdoors

10 km of mountain trails and a 4 km running and biking road laid out in two lanes, with benches, lighting along the route and bus stops for public transport.

02

Courts & play

A courts centre with tennis, basketball, sand beach volleyball and paddle tennis. For children, a playground, climbing wall and outdoor gym — plus a dogs’ garden, organic garden and farmers’ market.

03

Gardens & water

A serene Zen garden and dedicated yoga and meditation area, swimming pools, and a Mini Aquapark for escaping the summer heat.

04

Spa & wellbeing

An indoor pool, spa and massage centre, and separate sauna and steam rooms with ice-shower facilities. Indoor gardens run throughout.

05

Work & gathering

Bookable lounges, a co-working space and a conference hall that doubles as a cinema room. A restaurant, a bar and mist lounge, and a roof bar with skylight star watching.

06

At your doorstep

24/7 A&E healthcare, supermarket, coffee shop and bakery, bank and ATM, pharmacy, car rental, bike and scooter rental, and beauticians.
