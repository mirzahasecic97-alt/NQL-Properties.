The Project

Branded living, under one management

A new-generation branded living project in Northern Cyprus, featuring a strategic partnership between three independent companies: Habitat, Arkin and Dedeman.

Designed as an integrated master plan for living, rental income and capital growth, the project consists of 600 branded residences operated under unified management to ensure hotel-standard services and lasting value.

The community includes a 1.4 km grand boulevard with cascading waterfalls and a 3.5 km cycling and walking path.

Habitat Premium

Branded residences, professionally let

Habitat Premium is a partnership between Evergreen Development Group, Arkin Group, Dedeman Hotels & Resorts and Turkish Airlines Miles&Smiles — fully furnished residences built and operated to international hotel standards, with hotel operations connected to more than 70 international booking platforms and travel agencies to maximise occupancy through the year.

Payment Terms

35% down, 36 months interest-free

Habitat Premium is sold direct from the developer with a 35% down payment and the balance spread across 36 months, interest-free, through to completion in December 2028.

Purchase taxes in North Cyprus are calculated on 70% of the declared price and total approximately 14.5% of that figure — see the full cost breakdown.
