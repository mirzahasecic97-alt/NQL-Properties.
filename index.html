About NQL

The home, the season, and the evenings you will talk about for years.

NQL began by finding homes abroad for Nordic families, through local experts we had vetted ourselves. Today we also rent villas and yachts and arrange the dinners, days and journeys that make a place yours. Whether you are buying, renting or simply arriving, one team acts only for you, from the first call to the last evening.
