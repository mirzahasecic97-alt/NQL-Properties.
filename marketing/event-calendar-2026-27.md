# Where to be: the event calendar we market around

Chosen by Mirza on 30 October 2026. One story a fortnight, each one a place we
rent in, each one linking to the Rent form for that place. Dates marked ~ are
the usual window; confirm each one about three months out and fix the date
here.

**Rhythm.** Publish the first post 10 to 12 weeks before the event, a reminder
at 4 weeks, and a "last villas" note at 10 days. The very rich book peaks
early, so a post on the week of the event sells next year, not this one.

**Link.** Every post ends on the destination link, which opens the contact
form with Rent chosen and the place filled in:
`https://www.nqlgroup.com/contact?about=rent&place=<place>`. The place is
written exactly as on the Destinations page, for example
`Saint%20Jean%20Cap%20Ferrat%2C%20France`.

**Channels.** Instagram (one image, the place, the date, "from EUR 10,000 a
night"), the newsletter (one email a month, the next three events), WhatsApp to
past enquirers who asked about that region.

- One picture of the place, not of the event (we do not hold event rights).
- The date, the place, "villas from EUR 10,000 a night", and the link.
- Never the partner's name. The public wording is "an internationally
  award-winning luxury rental house among our partners".
