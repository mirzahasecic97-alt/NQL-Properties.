Outbound, set up and run for you

A sales agent that finds the right companies, writes to the right person in your voice, answers the replies and books the meeting. You read the first week; after that it runs. From NQL Group.

What it is

Software that works your top of funnel every day without a salesperson. It learns your business from your website, builds a list of companies that match your best customers, finds the decision maker, verifies the address, and sends a three-step sequence written in your own words. When someone answers, it sorts the reply, handles the simple ones, and hands you the people who want to talk.

What you get

  • A list of target companies in your markets, built from how you describe your best customers, refreshed as you go.
  • The right person at each one, with a verified email address. Guessed addresses are never written to.
  • Three emails per person in your voice, from a house style we write with you. Under 75 words each, one question, no sales language.
  • Replies sorted and answered: interested, objection, question, wrong person, out of office, unsubscribe. The awkward ones come to you.
  • Meetings booked and placed in your calendar or CRM.
  • A dashboard: what went out, who answered, what is waiting on you.

How it goes

1

One hour with you. Who buys from you, where, what the first email should say, and where meetings should land. We set up a mailbox on a sending domain that protects your main one.

2

Two weeks of warming. The new mailbox earns a reputation before the first cold email. Meanwhile the list is built and you read the first drafts.

3

The first week, with approval. Every email waits for your yes. You correct the voice once, at the source.

4

Then it runs. A slow daily ramp, caps, a stop if bounces rise. You see replies and meetings; we see to the rest.

The rules it keeps

  • Business-to-business only, with an opt-out in every email and a postal address where the law asks for one.
  • Consumer email is not sent in countries that require consent. We tell you which of your markets those are before we start.
  • Your main domain is never used for cold email.
  • No one is written to more than three times. A "no" is honoured at once, by machine, not by memory.
