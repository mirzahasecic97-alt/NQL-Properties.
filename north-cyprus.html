Cost of Purchase

Purchase costs

Purchase taxes in North Cyprus are calculated on 70% of the declared price, and total approximately 14.5% of that figure.

Cost Amount When
Contract registration fee 6% On signing
Stamp duty 0.5% On signing
Permission to Purchase (PTP) Half the North Cyprus minimum wage On signing
VAT 5% At handover
Title deed transfer fee 3% On full payment / completion
Transformer & generator connection £5,500 (fixed) At handover
Legal fees from £750 On appointment

Illustrative only and not tax advice. Figures should be confirmed with your lawyer before committing to a purchase.
