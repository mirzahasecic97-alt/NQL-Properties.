This policy sets out what happens to information you give us, in plain terms. It describes what this website actually does — not what a template says a website might do.

The short version:

  • —We collect what you type into a form. Nothing else.
  • —There is no analytics, no advertising pixel and no tracking cookie on this site.
  • —Your enquiry is stored in the EU and seen by our four staff.
  • —If we introduce you to a partner agency, we tell you who.
  • —Ask us to delete your data and we will, without asking why.

The rest of this page is the detail behind those five points.

NQL Properties is the trading name of NordicQL Real Estate, registered in Norway. We are the data controller for the information described here, which means we decide why it is collected and what happens to it.

03 What we do not collect

This is the part most privacy policies leave vague, so to be explicit:

  • —No analytics. No Google Analytics, no Plausible, no Matomo. We do not know how many people visit this site or which pages they read.
  • —No advertising pixels. No Meta pixel, no Google Ads tag, no LinkedIn or TikTok tracking. We cannot retarget you and do not try to.
  • —No tracking cookies. This website sets no cookies at all. That is why you have never seen a cookie banner here.
  • —No profiling and no automated decisions. A person reads every enquiry.
  • —No selling or renting of data. Ever, to anyone, for any price.

The only browser storage we use is on our internal staff login at /crm, which keeps a session token so our own team is not signed out every few minutes. It is not set for visitors.

Four companies are involved in getting your enquiry to us and keeping it safe. Each is bound by a data processing agreement and none may use your information for their own purposes.

Transfers to the United States rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. If you would rather your details never left the EU, email us instead of using the form and say so.

Partner agencies

We work with local agencies across the Mediterranean, and buying a property abroad usually means one of them handling the viewing and the paperwork. If your enquiry needs an agency involved, we will tell you which one and why before we pass anything on. We do not hand your details to a partner and hope for the best.

Fonts, images and maps

Some pages load fonts from Google, styling from a Tailwind CDN, images from Unsplash, and on a few property pages an embedded Google map. Loading any of these tells that company your IP address and roughly where you are. That is how the open web works, but you deserve to know it happens. None of it identifies you by name, and the Google map is the only one that may set a cookie of its own.

06 Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to do any of the following, free of charge. We answer within thirty days.

  • —See everything we hold about you. We will send it in full.
  • —Correct anything wrong.
  • —Have it deleted. We will not ask you to justify it.
  • —Object to us using it, at which point we stop unless we are legally required to keep it.
  • —Take it elsewhere, in a machine-readable file.
  • —Withdraw consent to the newsletter, from the link in any email or by asking us.

Email info@nordicql.com with what you want. You do not need to cite the law or use particular wording — “please delete my data” is enough.

If you think we have handled your information badly, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain directly to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet), or to the equivalent authority in the EEA country where you live.

07 Keeping it secure

  • —The site is served over HTTPS everywhere.
  • —Enquiries sit in a database that refuses every request without a valid staff login, enforced at the database itself rather than in the website.
  • —Only the four of us at NQL Properties have accounts, each with an individual password. Account creation is closed — nobody can register themselves.
  • —The keys that could read the database are held by our hosting provider and never appear in the website's code.

No system is perfect. If there is ever a breach that puts you at risk, we will tell you and the supervisory authority within 72 hours, as the law requires.

08 Children

This site is meant for adults buying property. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us something, tell us and we will delete it.

09 Changes to this policy

This version is dated 21 August 2026. If we change how we handle your information we will update this page and change that date. For anything significant — a new category of data, a new recipient — we will tell the people it affects directly rather than expecting them to re-read this page.

Something here unclear?

Ask us. A privacy policy nobody can follow is not transparency, and we would rather explain it than have you guess.
