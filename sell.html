For owners

Selling in the Mediterranean?
We already know who is buying.

Buyers from the Nordics and the United States come to NQL Properties before they start looking, with a budget, a region and a clear idea of the place they want. We come to your house, film the tour, take the photographs, and put it in front of them properly. No portal listing, no open house, no sign at the gate unless you want one.

Tell us about your property

The buyer comes first, then the house

Most agencies find a house and then look for a buyer. We work the other way round. People tell us what they want and what they can spend, we qualify them, and only then do we go looking. So when we bring someone to your door, they are not browsing. They have told us your region, your sort of house and your price, and we have checked that they mean it.

Our buyers are mostly Norwegian, Swedish, Danish, Icelandic and American. They buy second homes, places to retire to, and houses to let, in Italy above all, and across the Mediterranean.

What you get

  • A filmed house tour. We come to the property, walk it on camera and edit the film, so a buyer in Oslo or New York sees it the way you would show it.
  • Photographs and presentation. We take the pictures if you need them, and make sure the house looks the way it deserves to before anyone sees it.
  • Marketing where the buyers are. The tour and the pictures go to our buyer list and into our advertising in the Nordics and the United States, and to the local agencies we work with.
  • Qualified buyers only. Every person we introduce has given us a budget and a brief, and we have spoken to them.
  • Discretion. Off market if you prefer. Your address and your photographs go only to the buyer we have matched, never onto a listings page.
  • One point of contact. We handle the buyer from the first call to the viewing, in their language, in their time zone.
  • Your agent stays. If you already have an agency, we work alongside them. Many of our partners are the local agency that already has your house.

What it looks like

How it works

1

Tell us about the house

Where it is, what it is, what you want for it. The form below takes two minutes, or we call you.

2

We come and film it

A day at the property: the house tour on camera, photographs if you need them, and a word about anything worth fixing before buyers see it.

3

We market it

To the buyers already on our list who asked for a house like yours, in our advertising in the Nordics and the US, and to our partner agencies. Off market stays off market.

4

The buyer sees it

We present the house ourselves and arrange viewings only for buyers who have seen the film and still want to come.

5

You sell

Negotiation and completion run through your agent or a notary as usual. We stay with the buyer until the keys change hands.

What it costs

Nothing to register a property with us. The visit, the film and the marketing are agreed with you up front, in writing, before we book the trip. Our fee on the sale itself is agreed before the first viewing and paid only on completion.

We are a buyer’s agency. We will always tell you plainly whether we have someone for your house, and we would rather say no than waste a season of your time.

Tell us about your property

A person reads this, not an autoresponder. We will come back within a working day and tell you honestly whether we have a buyer who fits.

Your details are used to reply to you and to match your property with our buyers, nothing else, under our privacy policy. Nothing about your property is shown to anyone without your say so.
