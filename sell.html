For owners

Selling in the Mediterranean?
We already know who is buying.

We come to your house, film the tour, shoot a 360° walkthrough if you want one, and put it in front of Nordic and American buyers who are already looking.

Tell us about your property

What it looks like

We come to the house

A villa near Vasanello, north of Rome. One day on site, the house and the garden on camera, and the film went to our buyers, into our advertising and to our partner agencies the same week. A 360° tour lets a buyer walk every room from their own sofa.

How it works

1

Tell us about the house

Two minutes in the form below, or we call you.

2

We come and film it

The tour on camera, a 360° walkthrough if you want one, photographs if you need them, and the house looking its best.

3

We market it

To our buyers, our advertising in the Nordics and the US, and our partner agencies. Off market if you prefer.

4

You sell

We bring only buyers who have seen the film and still want to come. Completion runs through your agent or a notary as usual.

What it costs

Nothing to register a property with us. The visit, the film and the marketing are agreed with you in writing before we book the trip, and our fee on the sale is paid only on completion.

Tell us about your property

A person reads this, not an autoresponder. We will come back within a working day and tell you honestly whether we have a buyer who fits.

Your details are used to reply to you and to match your property with our buyers, nothing else, under our privacy policy. Nothing about your property is shown to anyone without your say so.
